__TITLE__ architecture study

PIO-style cores vs a software-allocated mesh: area and utilisation over the protocol mixes in the pack's arch/workloads.yaml. Tables: arch-study.md · terms and formulas: arch-guide.md · method and scope: ADR-0007.

Analytic estimates. Cell and SRAM areas come from the IHP PDK; block costs (core, units, FIFO control, links) are placeholders. Activity is estimated from each program's bit shares and an assumed traffic load. Allocation is static (load time). Generated by uv run pioscope arch --write; do not edit.

Architecture and allocation

Each block is one piece of hardware. Its colour is the program that uses it in the selected workload; the bar along its bottom is how busy it is (estimate). Hatched blocks are idle. Hover or focus a block for details.
